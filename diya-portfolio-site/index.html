About Me

I work at the intersection of data, business and brand, with experience across aerospace manufacturing, global wealth management, capital lending and beauty communications. Each taught me to approach problems differently, but the instinct stays the same: look beyond the obvious, question what the data is saying, and understand what it means for the decision at hand.

Mathematics and Business Administration at the University of Waterloo gave me the analytical foundation. Across Bombardier, Manulife and Viva Capital, I built dashboards, automated reporting and turned complex datasets into something teams could actually use.

At Stanley Communications I bring that same curiosity to beauty, working across Huda Beauty, Kylie Cosmetics, Typsy Beauty and Forever 52 on creator strategy, competitive research, campaign planning, product launches and brand communications.

Different industries, different problems, but the same approach: understand the evidence, find the opportunity, and make the answer useful.
